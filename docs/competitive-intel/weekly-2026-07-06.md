# Competitive Intelligence — Week of 2026-07-06

## 1. Academic / arXiv

### Notable

**Wotten & Kim — Multi-objective build orientation and topology optimization for fiber-reinforced AM**
*Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization*, 2026 — [link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4)

The most directly relevant academic work this week. Simultaneously optimizes build orientation and part topology in a multi-objective framework targeting cost (build height + overhang area + support volume + surface area) for fiber-reinforced composites (FRAM). Uses non-dominated sorting, structurally the same class of problem as orientIQ's Pareto front sweep.

**Why it matters:** Confirms the multi-objective + orientation framing is gaining traction in peer-reviewed literature. The fiber-reinforced angle is out of scope for orientIQ today (we don't model anisotropic material properties), but the objective decomposition mirrors ours closely. Worth reading the full paper to check whether their gradient approach is competitive with our closed-form Riemannian descent.

---

**arXiv 2606.16792 — "Novel insights into Pareto fronts in multiobjective topology optimization and a comparative study of scalarization strategies"**
[link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.16792)

June 2026 preprint. Covers scalarization strategies (weighted sum, ε-constraint, Chebyshev) for Pareto front construction in structural optimization — the same algorithmic territory as `pareto.py` and `pairwise_pareto()`.

**Why it matters:** Low competitive threat (topology optimization, not orientation), but useful as a reference for validating our scalarization approach and checking whether any new method outperforms the λ-sweep we use.

## 3. Startups & Industry

### Castor has shut down
[3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/319042/israeli-3d-printing-software-startup-castor-files-for-liquidation/) | [3D Printing Industry](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/castor-closes-after-eight-years-as-founder-confirms-companys-end-246199/)

Israeli AM software startup Castor (part-screening / AM feasibility analysis) has filed for liquidation in Tel Aviv after eight years, citing a post-2023 slowdown in enterprise AM investment. Raised ~$5.9M total; owes ~$2.3M.

**Why it matters:** Castor focused on *whether* to print a part additively, not *how* to orient it, so the product overlap was limited. But its shutdown confirms that pure-software AM analytics startups face a hard enterprise sales cycle right now. Relevant as a cautionary data point for orientIQ's go-to-market, and it frees up some customer attention from a company that was often mentioned alongside orientation tools.

**Atlas 3D / Sunata** — Acquired by Siemens in 2019, now embedded in NX/Teamcenter. No new 2026 activity found.
