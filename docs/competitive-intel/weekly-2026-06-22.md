## 1. Academic Papers

### Wotten & Kim (2026) — Multi-objective build orientation + topology optimization for fiber-reinforced AM
- **What it is:** Journal paper in *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization* (Springer, doi:10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4) proposing simultaneous minimization of compliance and support volume for fiber-reinforced AM, with build direction as a co-optimization variable alongside topology.
- **Why it matters:** Closest recent academic work to orientIQ's multi-objective framing. Key difference: they co-optimize topology (the shape changes), whereas orientIQ operates on a **fixed geometry** — the user's actual part. Their method is a design tool, not an orientation-finding tool for production parts. No threat to the core use case; confirms the research direction is credible.
- **Source:** [Springer link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4)

### "Combined topology and build orientation optimization for support minimization" (2025, Springer)
- **What it is:** Earlier companion paper in the same journal, also bundling topology and orientation.
- **Why it matters:** Same pattern as above — design-time tools, not production orientation finders. No direct competition.
- **Source:** [ACM/Springer mirror](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1007/s00158-025-04124-6)

### No fresh arXiv preprints found on Hopf fibration + 3D printing, Gauss map + AM, or pure orientation optimization
- Search returned papers from 2019–2022 only on those specific terms. orientIQ's Hopf-fibration reduction of SO(3) → S² remains unpublished in the AM context as of this search.

## 3. Startup / Commercial News

### Castor Technologies — filed for liquidation
- **What it is:** Israeli AM software startup (founded 2017, Tel Aviv) that analyzed CAD files to recommend AM vs. traditional manufacturing — including geometry-based part feasibility and orientation recommendations. Filed for liquidation; cited U.S. AM market downturn from early 2023, reduced R&D investment from public companies, and inability to raise further funding.
- **Why it matters:** **Direct competitor is dead.** Castor's part-analysis customers are now unserved and looking for alternatives. orientIQ's geometry-based optimization pitch is strengthened by Castor's failure to find a market — but the failure was commercial (AM market downturn, B2B sales cycle), not technical. Worth watching whether any acquirer picks up the IP or team.
- **Source:** [3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/319042/israeli-3d-printing-software-startup-castor-files-for-liquidation/)
