# Competitive Intelligence — Week of 2026-08-24

## Research Papers

### Notable

**"Multi-objective build orientation and topology optimization for cost and time minimization in fiber-reinforced additive manufacturing"**
Wotten & Kim, *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization* Vol. 69, 2026.
- **What it is:** Simultaneous topology + build-direction optimization targeting compliance and support volume in fiber-reinforced FDM.
- **Why it matters:** Addresses the same overhang/height trade-off space as orient-opt but from a topology-first angle; uses gradient-based optimization on combined geometry+orientation variables. The joint optimization framing is more powerful than post-hoc orientation search, but also much more expensive and tightly coupled to a specific material model. Not an API-style library; academic code only.
- **Source:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4

**"A comprehensive review of support structure minimization in additive manufacturing: design, orientation, optimization, and process strategies"**
Chaudhary et al., *Int. J. Advanced Manufacturing Technology*, 2026.
- **What it is:** Survey paper covering design-for-AM, orientation optimization, and support elimination strategies across FDM/SLA/SLS/metal.
- **Why it matters:** Signals the field is now mature enough to warrant a comprehensive review; likely to cite Gauss-map / sphere-search methods. Worth reading to see how orient-opt's Hopf-fibration approach fits the broader landscape and what gaps reviewers call out.
- **Source:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00170-026-18633-1

**"Integrating Orientation Optimization and Thermal Distortion Prediction in LPBF"**
PMC 2026 (open-access).
- **What it is:** Couples orientation search with thermal distortion simulation for laser powder-bed fusion (metal printing).
- **Why it matters:** Thermal distortion is the dominant objective for metal AM — orient-opt currently targets FDM/SLA objectives (overhang area, build height, surface quality). This paper's approach is a reminder that metal AM has a distinct objective function where orient-opt's current model would not transfer without a thermal-distortion term.
- **Source:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12654590/

## Slicer Software Releases

No new version releases with orientation or support changes were found for Cura, PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, Chitubox, Netfabb, or Materialise Magics in the last 7 days. Searches returned only evergreen feature-comparison articles and older changelogs.

**Context:** Auto-orientation is now a commodity feature in most slicers (Lychee, Anycubic/OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer all ship one-click orient). These heuristics remain geometry-agnostic — no slicer exposes multi-objective Pareto fronts or functional surface constraints. The gap orient-opt fills is still open.

## Startups & Commercial Players

**Castor Technologies — confirmed closed (mid-2025)**
- **What happened:** Filed for court-ordered liquidation in Tel Aviv District Court in June 2025; website is offline. The company owed ~ILS 8.6 M (~$2.3 M USD). Founded 2015, focused on AM feasibility analysis and geometry-based part selection.
- **Why it matters:** One fewer direct-geometry-analysis competitor; Castor's closure leaves a gap in the "should I print this part?" tooling space, but their orientation optimization was secondary to their cost/feasibility focus. Potential acqui-hire opportunity if any of their algorithmic staff are available.
- **Source:** https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/castor-closes-after-eight-years-as-founder-confirms-companys-end-246199/

**Atlas 3D / Sunata (now Siemens)**
- No new announcements this week. Sunata is now part of Siemens' AM portfolio. Their differentiator is thermal-distortion-driven orientation for metal AM (DMLS/LPBF), not FDM geometry. Not a direct competitor to orient-opt's current FDM/SLA scope.
- **Source:** https://ideacenter.nd.edu/news-events/news/notre-dame-startup-success-siemens-expands-additive-manufacturing-portfolio-with-acquisition-of-atlas-3d/
