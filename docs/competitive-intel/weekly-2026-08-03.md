## 1. arXiv Papers

### Notable

**arXiv:2606.16792 — "Novel insights into Pareto fronts in multiobjective topology optimization and a comparative study of scalarization strategies"**
_De Weer, Sigmund, Eichfelder — June 2026_

- **What it is:** Theoretical analysis of Pareto front geometry in bi-objective topology optimization. Shows the frontier consists of locally disconnected pieces (nonconvex, non-contiguous), and benchmarks weighted-sum vs. ε-constraint vs. Pascoletti-Serafini scalarization. Finds Pascoletti-Serafini gives more uniform coverage; weighted-sum clusters at extremes.
- **Competitive relevance:** Directly touches orientIQ's Pareto front output. The finding that Pareto fronts in topology/geometry optimization are structurally nonconvex and potentially disconnected validates why a sweep over λ ∈ [0,1] can miss regions — a known limitation of orientIQ's current `pareto_front()` approach. Pascoletti-Serafini scalarization could be a future enhancement for denser, more uniform Pareto coverage. Monitor this group's follow-up work.
- **Source:** [arxiv.org/abs/2606.16792](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.16792)
