## 1. Academic / arXiv

### Wotten & Kim — Multi-objective build orientation + topology optimization (fiber-reinforced AM)
**What it is:** Published in *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization* (vol. 69, 2026). Jointly optimizes build direction and topology for fiber-reinforced AM, minimizing compliance and support structure volume simultaneously. Treats support minimization as a co-objective, not a post-hoc constraint.  
**Competitive relevance:** Directly overlaps with orientIQ's multi-objective framing (overhang + build height + surface quality). Key difference: their approach couples topology design and orientation together (design-for-AM from scratch), whereas orientIQ operates on a fixed mesh. No threat to the fixed-mesh use case; confirms multi-objective orientation is an active research direction. Watch for a follow-on tool or open-source release.  
**Source:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4

### Crispo & Kim — Topology + build orientation cost reduction (four differentiable cost proxies)
**What it is:** Published in *Progress in Additive Manufacturing*, 2026. Models AM cost via four differentiable objectives (build height, surface area, overhang area, support volume) and optimizes topology and orientation jointly using gradient-based methods.  
**Competitive relevance:** The four-objective decomposition mirrors orientIQ's objective set almost exactly. Their gradient-based approach is on SO(3) embedded in a topology loop; orientIQ's S²-reduction (Hopf fibration) is mathematically tighter for the fixed-mesh sub-problem. No open-source release found; academic only for now.  
**Source:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40964-025-01520-8

### No hits this week: Hopf fibration + 3D printing, Gauss map + AM
Nothing new on arXiv using these exact terms in the past 7 days. orientIQ's S² search-space reduction remains a differentiator not yet appearing in the literature.

## 2. Slicer Releases

### Autodesk Netfabb 2026 — True Shape Packer
**What it is:** Netfabb 2026 ships a new voxel-based 2D/3D packing algorithm ("True Shape Packer") that respects actual part geometry, supports no-build zones, handles rotation, and scales to multi-platform serial production. Automatable via Lua scripting.  
**Competitive relevance:** Packing ≠ orientation optimization, but denser packing indirectly competes for the same "print efficiency" value proposition. No orientation or support minimization improvements noted in this release. Low direct threat; note for completeness.  
**Source:** https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/netfabb-forum/autodesk-netfabb-2026-is-now-available/td-p/13437539
