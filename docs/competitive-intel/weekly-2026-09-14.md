# Competitive Intelligence — Week of 2026-09-14

## 1. arXiv / Research

### Notable (not last 7 days, but recent enough to flag)

**Crispo & Kim — "Comprehensive additive manufacturing cost reduction through simultaneous topology and build orientation optimization"**
*Progress in Additive Manufacturing (Springer), 2026*
- **What:** Simultaneously co-optimizes part topology AND build orientation using four cost proxies: build height, surface area, overhang area, and support volume.
- **Why it matters:** Directly adjacent to orientIQ's objective formulation. Key difference: they solve the joint topology+orientation problem (heavier, design-phase tool), while orientIQ fixes geometry and solves orientation only (much faster, any mesh). If their approach becomes popular as a DfAM tool it validates the problem space without cannibalizing post-design use cases. Worth reading the method section for overlap with our objective weights.
- [Link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40964-025-01520-8)

**arXiv:2607.13174 — "Towards end-to-end optimization in multimaterial 3D printing"**
*Luo et al., submitted July 14 2026*
- **What:** Neural-network constitutive models + FEM topology optimization for simultaneous macro-topology and material distribution in soft robotics parts.
- **Why it matters:** Not an orientation paper — no competitive threat. Confirms neural/differentiable approaches are gaining traction in AM optimization broadly; orientIQ's closed-form analytical gradients remain a speed/interpretability advantage for the orientation subproblem.
- [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13174)

**No papers found this week** matching "Hopf fibration + 3D printing", "Gauss map + additive manufacturing", or "build direction optimization" with submission dates in the 7-day window (Sept 7–14). The space remains quiet on those specific search terms.

## 2. Slicer Releases

**Netfabb 2026 — True Shape Packing (released earlier in 2026)**
- **What:** Complete ground-up rewrite of Netfabb's nesting engine. Replaces bounding-box nesting with a voxel-based algorithm that packs actual part geometry across multiple build platforms simultaneously.
- **Why it matters:** This is *packing* (multi-part placement), not orientation optimization — it assumes orientation is already decided. Does not compete directly. However, it raises the baseline expectation for AM software quality and could make Siemens/Autodesk ecosystem more attractive for production workflows that currently bolt on a separate orientation step.
- [Autodesk Community announcement](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/netfabb-forum/autodesk-netfabb-2026-is-now-available/td-p/13437539) | [3D Printing Industry](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/autodesk-updates-netfabb-with-enhanced-workflow-tools-and-new-3mf-support-in-2026-release-238996/)

**Cura / PrusaSlicer / Bambu Studio / Chitubox / Materialise Magics**
- No releases or changelogs touching auto-orient or support optimization found in the past 7 days. Auto-orient in Bambu Studio (minimum overhang area heuristic) and Lychee Slicer remain the consumer-slicer state of the art — simple single-objective, no Pareto, no functional surface constraints.
