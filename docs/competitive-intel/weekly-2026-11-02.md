## 1. Academic / arXiv

### arXiv:2609.15710 — ML-based build orientation prediction for SLM dental parts
**What it is:** September 2026 preprint comparing rotation representations (quaternion, 6D continuous, axis-angle) and direct unit-vector regression for predicting optimal SLA/SLM build orientation from CAD geometry.  
**Why it matters:** First ML approach to orientation that treats the output as a point on S² via direct vector regression — the same search-space reduction orientIQ uses analytically. If the method generalises beyond dental CAD it could be a methodology overlap to watch; currently domain-specific and requires training data.  
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15710

### Springer SMO 2026 — Multi-objective build orientation + topology for fiber-reinforced parts
**What it is:** Two papers (Wotten & Kim; Ray & Kim) in *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization* jointly optimize print orientation, stacking sequence, and topology for CFRP parts. One reports 68.8% manufacturing-cost and 61.2% print-time reduction vs. a fixed-orientation baseline.  
**Why it matters:** Targets fiber-reinforced / metal AM at the topology level — out of orientIQ's scope today (FDM/SLA polymer). Validates multi-objective orientation framing but uses FEM simulation, not surface-normal geometry. Not a direct threat; watch if orientIQ expands to CFRP.  
**Sources:**  
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4  
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04308-8

### No Gauss map / Hopf fibration + AM papers found
Nothing in the last 7 days uses the Gauss-map or Hopf-fibration framing for 3D-printing orientation. orientIQ's mathematical approach remains differentiated.
