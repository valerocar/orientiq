### Adjacent paper of note (multi-axis, different problem)

**INF-3DP: Implicit Neural Fields for Collision-Free Multi-Axis 3D Printing**  
*Submitted Sept 2, 2025 (arXiv:2509.05345); published ACM TOG 2026*  
- **What it is:** Uses implicit neural fields (signed distance functions + quaternion fields) for 5-axis toolpath planning and collision-free motion scheduling — not single-axis orientation selection.  
- **Why it matters:** Different problem class (multi-axis CNC-style motion vs. fixed-bed FDM/SLA orientation). No overlap with orient-opt's search space reduction. The quaternion field usage is superficially similar but serves path-planning, not Hopf-section orientation encoding.  
- **Verdict:** Monitor for citation network; not a competitive threat.  
- [arxiv.org/abs/2509.05345](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05345)

### Academic trend (not this week, but relevant context)

**Multi-objective build orientation + topology optimization for fiber-reinforced AM**  
*Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization, 2026*  
- **What it is:** Simultaneous topology optimization and build orientation selection for fiber-reinforced composite parts, minimizing compliance + support structures.  
- **Why it matters:** Reflects the dominant academic direction: design-time co-optimization of part geometry and orientation. This is upstream of orient-opt's use case (given a fixed mesh, find the best orientation). If design-time tools proliferate, they reduce the population of "legacy mesh, need to orient it" users — but they also validate the importance of orientation as a first-class variable.  
- **Verdict:** Watch the design-for-AM space; not a direct competitor to pre-print orientation tools.  
- [link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4)

## 3. Startup / Commercial Landscape

### Castor — closed

- **What happened:** Castor (Tel Aviv; CAD-file analysis + orientation/part suitability software) filed for bankruptcy in July 2025 and ceased operations in November 2025.  
- **Why it matters:** Castor was the closest public-facing competitor in the "geometry-based orientation optimization" space. Its closure removes a benchmark and a potential acquirer/partner, but also confirms the market hasn't yet found a clean B2B motion for standalone orientation SaaS.

## Assessment

orient-opt's core moat — Hopf-fibration search-space reduction, analytical S² gradients, Pareto front UI — remains unduplicated in any public tool or paper this week. The Castor closure is a meaningful signal: the standalone orientation-SaaS model failed at Series A scale, suggesting orient-opt should target integration into existing workflows (slicer plugins, CAM pipelines) rather than a standalone commercial product.
