## 3. Startups & Commercial Tools

### Castor Technologies — **Shut down (July 2025)**
- **What:** Israeli startup offering AM decision-support and orientation tooling; had a partnership with Evonik.
- **Why it matters:** Castor is no longer a competitor. The company entered court-ordered liquidation in July 2025 after funding dried up. Its niche was eroded by CAD/ERP tools adding basic AM manufacturability checks. This is a cautionary data point for the market segment, but also removes a direct competitor.
- Source: [3DPrint.com 2025 year-end review](https://3dprint.com/322764/who-merged-who-was-acquired-and-who-disappeared-in-3d-printing-in-2025/) · [3dprintingjournal.com](https://www.3dprintingjournal.com/p/when-a-business-reaches-the-end-of)

### Atlas 3D / Sunata (Siemens)
- **What:** GPU-accelerated cloud tool targeting DMLS metal printing. Acquired by Siemens in 2019; now part of Siemens' AM portfolio.
- **Why it matters:** Sunata's thermal-circuit-network approach is focused on metal powder-bed fusion distortion, not FDM/SLA overhang minimization. Different target market from orientIQ. No 2026 product updates found.
- Source: [Voxelmatters acquisition announcement](https://www.voxelmatters.com/siemens-expands-additive-manufacturing-portfolio-through-acquisition-of-atlas-3d/) · [Additive Manufacturing Media](https://www.additivemanufacturing.media/products/atlas-3ds-sunata-am-software-automates-orientation)

## Takeaways

1. **No immediate research threats.** The Hopf-fibration + analytical-gradient approach is not being replicated in recent public work.
2. **Castor is gone.** Reduces competitive pressure in the decision-support/orientation software niche.
3. **Multi-axis printing** (arXiv 2502.20343 and others) is a long-horizon threat to the entire "pre-orientation" category — worth revisiting quarterly.
4. **Slicers remain heuristic-only** for orientation; orientIQ's mathematical rigor (Pareto fronts, constraint handling, S² gradient descent) is not matched by any slicer release this week.
