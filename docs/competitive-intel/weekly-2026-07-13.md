## arXiv / Academic

**1. Multi-objective build orientation + topology optimization (Springer SMO, 2026)**
- **What:** Wotten & Kim, "Multi-objective build orientation and topology optimization for cost and time minimization in fiber-reinforced additive manufacturing," *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization* vol. 69 (2026). Simultaneously optimizes compliance, build orientation, and support structure volume for fiber-reinforced AM.
- **Why it matters:** Directly overlaps orientIQ's multi-objective Pareto approach. The paper treats orientation as a co-design variable alongside topology — a more coupled formulation than orientIQ's current fixed-geometry optimization. No threat to orientIQ's library positioning (it's academic, not a tool), but worth reading for the Pareto front methodology.
- **Source:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4

**2. Pareto fronts in multi-objective topology optimization (arXiv 2606.16792, June 2026)**
- **What:** Academic study on non-dominated sorting and scalarization strategies for multi-objective topology optimization. Not AM-specific but directly relevant to the math orientIQ uses.
- **Why it matters:** Low competitive threat; confirms orientIQ's Pareto-front approach is actively researched. Scalarization comparisons could inform refinement of the `pareto.py` weighting strategy.
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.16792

**No new arXiv papers found this week combining "Hopf fibration" + "3D printing" or "Gauss map" + "additive manufacturing."** orientIQ's S²-reduction insight remains unpublished in the open literature.

## Slicer Releases

**3. PrusaSlicer 2.9.6 (released June 29, 2026 — just before this window)**
- **What:** Maintenance release; PrusaSlicer 3.0 remains unreleased as of July 13. Auto-orient (optimize orientation) still exists only for SLA (MSLA) printers, not FDM. Open GitHub issue #13091 requesting FDM auto-orient has no committed timeline.
- **Why it matters:** The most widely used open-source FDM slicer still has no native orientation optimizer for FDM. This is orientIQ's most direct integration opportunity — the gap is acknowledged and unfilled.
- **Sources:** https://forum.prusa3d.com/forum/prusaslicer/prusaslicer-3-0-release-date/ · https://github.com/prusa3d/PrusaSlicer/issues/13091

**4. Bambu Studio V2.8.0**
- **What:** Added Assembly Guide generation and counterbore hole bridging. No orientation optimization features mentioned.
- **Why it matters:** Bambu is the fastest-growing consumer FDM ecosystem. Still no auto-orient beyond manual rotation — no threat, potential integration target.
- **Source:** https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/software/bambu-studio/release

**5. Materialise Magics 30**
- **What:** 2026 release adds Label Planning, Honeycomb Lattice controls, Voronoi Beam Lattice, and ID-based licensing. No orientation-specific changes announced.
- **Why it matters:** Magics has long had basic auto-orient for metal AM (powder-bed). No indication they are advancing the algorithm. No new threat.
- **Source:** https://help.materialise.com/en_US/manuals-magics/magics-30

## Startups

**6. Castor Technologies — liquidated (mid-2025)**
- **What:** Israeli AM part-selection/orientation startup filed for liquidation in mid-2025 due to a broader US 3D printing market downturn starting 2023.
- **Why it matters:** Removes one direct competitor in geometry-based AM optimization. Customers that relied on Castor for orientation analysis are now unserved.
- **Source:** https://3dprint.com/319042/israeli-3d-printing-software-startup-castor-files-for-liquidation/

**Atlas 3D / Sunata:** Acquired by Siemens in 2019; no new standalone activity found this week. Now embedded in Siemens NX AM workflow (metal DMLS only).

**AMPower:** No relevant announcements found this week.

## Patents

**7. US Patent 12,343,928 — "Build orientation for additive manufacturing of complex structures"**
- **What:** Patent covering orientation selection that enables internal-cavity geometries to print without support structures, using a constraint-satisfaction approach over maze-like internal channels.
- **Why it matters:** Scope is narrow (internal cavities, not general multi-objective orientation). Does not conflict with orientIQ's S²-gradient approach, but is a data point that Siemens/large OEMs are actively patenting in this space.
- **Source:** https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/12343928
