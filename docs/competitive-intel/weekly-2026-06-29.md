## 2. Slicer Release Notes (June 22–29)

Three slicers shipped on June 25, 2026. None touched their orientation algorithms.

| Slicer | Version | Date | Relevant to orientIQ? |
|---|---|---|---|
| PrusaSlicer | 2.9.6 final | Jun 25 | No — introduces ColorMix (filament blending); no orientation or support changes. [GitHub](https://github.com/prusa3d/PrusaSlicer/releases) |
| Cura | 5.14.0-alpha.0 | Jun 25 | Marginal — adds "support painting" (user paints where supports go/don't go). Manual, not algorithmic. No auto-orient changes. [GitHub](https://github.com/Ultimaker/Cura/releases) |
| Bambu Studio | 2.8.0 beta | Jun 25 | No — adds counterbore hole bridging modes and Assembly Guide generation. No auto-orient changes. [GitHub](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/releases) |

**Cura's support painting** is the closest thing to a competitive signal: it gives users explicit control over support exclusion zones, which overlaps with orientIQ's `no_support_face_colors` constraint mechanism. Cura's version is manual (paint it yourself); orientIQ's is geometry-driven and coupled to the optimizer. Not a threat today, but worth watching if Cura automates the painting step via mesh region detection.

**Near-miss outside window:** Materialise Magics 30.0 (June 15) added implicit geometry support generation. No orientation algorithm changes. [Materialise](https://www.materialise.com/en/industrial/software/magics-data-build-preparation/whats-new)
