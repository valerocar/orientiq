## Academic Papers

### 1. Multi-objective build orientation + topology optimization for fiber-reinforced AM
**What it is:** Wotten & Kim, *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization* (2026, DOI 10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4). Simultaneously optimizes build orientation and topology for fiber-reinforced AM, minimizing compliance and support structure volume as competing objectives.

**Why it matters:** Directly overlaps orientIQ's multi-objective Pareto approach. The coupling of topology and orientation is more general than orientIQ's current scope (orientIQ takes geometry as fixed), but the objective set — support volume, build height, overhang — is nearly identical. Worth reading to see if their sphere-parameterization differs from the Hopf/S² approach. Not a product threat yet; academic paper with no associated tool.

**Source:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4
