### Bambu Studio 2.7.1 — June 1, 2026
**Summary:** Gradient Color Curve Control, Liveview Full-Screen, Alternate Extra Wall.  
**Orientation/support changes:** None.  
**Relevance:** Bambu's "auto-orientation" (tests 4 discrete rotation angles: 0°, 45°, 90°, 135°)

algorithmic depth.

improvements: users can now rotate the entire support grid and control support orientation relative

new orientation-search algorithm.  
**Relevance:** Low. Netfabb's support improvements are manual/interactive; they don't compete
with automated multi-objective orientation search.  
**Source:** [autodesk-netfabb-2026-is-now-available](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/netfabb-forum/autodesk-netfabb-2026-is-now-available/td-p/13437539)

## Area 4 — Patents

**No new applications or grants in the May 25 – June 1 window** were identified through
USPTO/Google Patents searches for "print orientation", "build direction", or "multi-objective
additive manufacturing optimization".

**Two recently-granted US patents noted (grant dates unconfirmed as this week):**

1. **US 12403662** — *"Determining build orientation to minimize thermal distortion"*  
   Claims a method for computing the build orientation that minimizes predicted thermal distortion
   in metal AM (likely Siemens Digital Industries, original filing Dec 17, 2020).  
   **Relevance:** Thermal distortion is not in orientIQ's current objective set; no IP conflict.
   Could indicate Siemens is fortifying its Netfabb/NX AM moat around metal-process simulation.  
   **Source:** [image-ppubs.uspto.gov/…/12403662](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/12403662)

2. **US 12343928** — *"Build orientation for additive manufacturing of complex structures"*  
   Claims a method for establishing aligned, secondary, and optimized build orientations for
   complex multi-surface components.  
   **Relevance:** Focuses on geometric alignment heuristics for machined-feature surfaces, not
   gradient-based optimization or Pareto trade-off analysis. Overlaps loosely with orientIQ's
   constraint-surface tagging but is procedurally rather than optimally oriented.  
   **Source:** [image-ppubs.uspto.gov/…/12343928](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/12343928)
