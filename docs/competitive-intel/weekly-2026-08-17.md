## 1. Academic / arXiv

### [NOTABLE] Multi-objective build orientation + topology optimization for fiber-reinforced AM
**Wotten & Kim, _Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization_, 2026**
- Simultaneous minimization of structural compliance and support volume for fiber-reinforced parts; uses a weighted multi-objective formulation over build direction and topology together.
- Why it matters: Validates orientIQ's core premise (multi-objective orientation optimization reduces support material without sacrificing structural goals). The key difference: their framework is coupled with topology optimization (design changes the geometry simultaneously), making it more of an academic DfAM tool than a standalone orient-then-print library. orientIQ's S²-reduction via Hopf fibration and closed-form gradients are not used here. Not a direct product threat, but signals growing academic legitimacy for the problem space.
- [Link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4)

### [LOW PRIORITY] Simultaneous topology + build orientation for AM cost reduction
**_Progress in Additive Manufacturing_, 2026**
- Models AM cost using four physical properties: build height, surface area, overhang area, support volume; optimizes topology and orientation jointly.
- Why it matters: Same four objective components orientIQ tracks. Confirms the field is converging on these metrics. Academic; no library or tool released.
- [Link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40964-025-01520-8)

### [LOW PRIORITY] Pareto fronts in multiobjective topology optimization (arXiv 2606.16792)
- Comparative study of scalarization strategies for Pareto fronts in topology optimization.
- Why it matters: Tangential — orientIQ already implements non-dominated sorting and pairwise Pareto. No new algorithm threat. Good for literature reference.
- [Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.16792)

**No arXiv papers found in the last 7 days specifically on Hopf fibration + 3D printing, Gauss map + AM, or support minimization as a standalone orientation library.**

## 3. Startup / Industry News

### [NOTABLE] CASTOR officially closed
- The Israeli AM analysis startup (founded 2017) confirmed shutdown; had filed for liquidation in Tel Aviv District Court in mid-2025.
- Why it matters: CASTOR was one of the few companies doing geometry-based AM analysis including orientation recommendation and part identification. Its exit removes a competitor in the geometry-first approach. The cause appears to be market-timing and enterprise sales friction, not a technical failure — worth noting for orientIQ's own go-to-market assumptions. Opportunity: former CASTOR customers are now without a solution.
- [Link](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/castor-closes-after-eight-years-as-founder-confirms-companys-end-246199/)
