### Two 2026 journal papers worth tracking (published earlier this year, surfaced now):

**Multi-objective build orientation + topology optimization for fiber-reinforced AM**
- Wotten & Kim, *Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization* 69(3), March 9, 2026.
- Simultaneously minimizes compliance and support structures for continuous fiber parts; claims 68.8% cost reduction and 61.2% print-time reduction vs. fixed-orientation baseline on a bracket.
- **Why it matters:** Direct overlap with orientIQ's multi-objective Pareto framing. Uses FEA-coupled topology optimization (heavier, offline) rather than orientIQ's lightweight S²/Fibonacci approach. Not a real-time tool. Academic, not productized yet.
- [Link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4)

**Comprehensive cost reduction via simultaneous topology + build orientation optimization**
- Crispo & Kim, *Progress in Additive Manufacturing* 11, February 3, 2026.
- Objectives include build height, surface area, overhang area, and support structure volume — the same objective set orientIQ uses.
- **Why it matters:** Confirms the objective set orientIQ chose is the academic consensus. Their method couples topology optimization (changes part geometry); orientIQ optimizes orientation of a fixed mesh — different use case, but same objective vocabulary.
- [Link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40964-025-01520-8)

## 3. Startups / Commercial Tools

**CASTOR — DEFUNCT**
- Israeli startup CASTOR has filed for court-ordered liquidation in Tel Aviv District Court; debt ~ILS 8.6M (~$2.3M).
- **Why it matters:** Castor was a geometry-based AM analysis platform backed by Xerox (raised ~$6–12M). Its collapse removes one player from the "should I 3D print this part?" screening market. Castor did part screening/cost analysis, not build-orientation optimization specifically — but its closure signals continued difficulty monetizing standalone AM software, a market risk orientIQ faces.
- [Shutdown report](https://3druck.com/en/programs/castor-ends-operations-after-eight-years-software-pioneer-for-am-analysis-closes-43151216/)
