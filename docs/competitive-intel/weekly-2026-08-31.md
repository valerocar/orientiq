## 1. arXiv / Academic Papers

**Nothing from Aug 24–31 matched the target keywords.** Most recent adjacent findings:

### [Low relevance] "Towards end-to-end optimization in multimaterial 3D printing" — arXiv:2607.13174 (Jul 14, 2026)
- ML-based approach treating multimaterial AM as a differentiable end-to-end pipeline.
- **Competitive read:** Uses learned models rather than closed-form geometry. Doesn't touch orientation search directly; focuses on material distribution. Not a direct threat, but signals growing interest in differentiable AM pipelines.
- [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13174)

### [Watch] "Multi-objective build orientation and topology optimization for cost and time minimization in fiber-reinforced additive manufacturing" — Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization, 2026
- Simultaneous topology + orientation co-optimization for fiber-reinforced FDM, targeting compliance and support minimization.
- **Competitive read:** Addresses multi-objective orientation search in the same problem space as orientIQ, but scoped to fiber-reinforced parts and coupled with topology. Gradient-based on a different representation (not S²). Validates the multi-objective angle; not a drop-in replacement for what orientIQ does with arbitrary meshes.
- [Link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4)

### [Low relevance] "Comprehensive additive manufacturing cost reduction through simultaneous topology and build orientation optimization" — Progress in Additive Manufacturing, 2025/2026
- Uses build height, surface area, overhang area, and support volume as cost proxies; all derived as differentiable functions for gradient-based optimization.
- **Competitive read:** Objective decomposition very similar to orientIQ's formulation. Coupled with topology optimization (different scope), but the cost model overlaps directly. Worth reading in full.
- [Link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40964-025-01520-8)

## 2. Slicer Releases

### [Moderate relevance] Netfabb 2026.0 — True Shape Packer (released Apr 22, 2026)
- New packing command supporting 2D and 3D nesting with "true shape" (actual mesh geometry) rather than bounding-box approximations. Includes duplicate-aware packing, high configurability, and Lua scripting automation.
- **Competitive read:** orientIQ doesn't do packing yet, but this raises the baseline expectation for AM software. Autodesk is investing in geometry-aware build preparation. If orientIQ ever adds multi-part packing, this is the benchmark to beat.
- [Autodesk Community announcement](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/netfabb-forum/autodesk-netfabb-2026-is-now-available/td-p/13437539) · [YouTube walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pAvcX5f78g)

### [Low relevance] Lychee Slicer 7.6.x (Jan–Feb 2026)
- Improvements to hollowing and auto-support export; no changes to core auto-orientation algorithm reported.
- **Competitive read:** Lychee's auto-orient is cited as the best among consumer slicers for resin printing. No algorithmic advancement this cycle; gap vs. orientIQ's Riemannian gradient descent remains wide.
- [Changelog](https://mango3d.io/changelogs/lychee-slicer-7-6-0)

### Bambu Studio / Cura / PrusaSlicer
- No orientation-specific releases found for Aug 24–31. Bambu's auto-orient remains a basic minimum-overhang-area heuristic (single-objective, no Pareto, no constraints).
