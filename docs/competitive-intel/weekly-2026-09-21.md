# Competitive Intelligence — Week of 2026-09-21

Coverage window: September 14–21, 2026.

---

## Summary

Quiet week. No arXiv papers on Hopf fibration / Gauss map approaches to print orientation appeared, and no major slicer shipped an orientation-specific release. The most notable structural fact is that Castor Technologies is definitively gone (liquidation confirmed 2025), and PrusaSlicer 3.0 alpha is creeping toward a stable release with no orientation features called out. Two Springer papers from earlier in 2026 (not this week) are flagged below as background reading.

## 1. arXiv — This Week (Sept 14–21)

**No directly relevant papers found this week.**

Two September 2026 arXiv submissions appeared in the AM space:

- [arXiv:2609.17104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17104) — "Extending high value components performances with Additive Manufacturing: application to naval applications" (Sep 15). Process/materials focus, no orientation work.

**Notable 2026 papers published before this week** (for awareness):

No papers this week or this year have used the Hopf fibration or Gauss map as a search space reduction for FDM/SLA orientation. orientIQ's mathematical framing remains unpublished in the academic literature.

Sunata/Siemens focuses on metal DMLS thermal distortion — a different problem than orientIQ's overhang/height/quality multi-objective formulation.
