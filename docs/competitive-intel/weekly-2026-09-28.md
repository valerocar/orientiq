# Competitive Intelligence — Week of 2026-09-28

## 1. arXiv / Academic Papers

### New This Week

**arXiv 2609.15710 — "Predicting build orientation for SLM dental parts: a comparison of rotation representations and direct vector regression"**
*Submitted 14 Sep 2026*

- **What:** ML approach to orientation prediction: ResNet-50 + PointNeXt-S trained on ~2,400 patient-specific dental parts, testing 13 rotation representations across six SO(3) parameterizations and seven S² representations. Best result: octahedral map, mean geodesic error 10.6° with test-time augmentation.
- **Why it matters:** Directly validates orientIQ's choice of S² as the natural search space — the paper's benchmark shows S²-native representations outperform generic SO(3) ones. The approach is complementary (supervised learning from labeled data) rather than competitive (no analytical gradients, no Pareto, no constraint support). Dental/metal SLM is a vertical worth watching for future customer segments.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15710

---

**arXiv 2609.02413 — "WildFab: Multi-Axis 3D Printing from Models in the Wild" (SIGGRAPH Asia 2026)**
*Submitted 2 Sep 2026*

- **What:** Pipeline for support-free multi-axis FDM using neural UDF + generalized winding number fields. Targets robotic 5-axis printing; handles non-manifold/solid-shell geometry without repair. Demonstrates physical fabrication on a robot arm.
- **Why it matters:** Multi-axis printing sidesteps the orientation problem entirely for some geometries — if 5-axis printers become mainstream, demand for 2-axis orientation optimization could shrink in the long run. Near-term: no commercial threat; this is research-grade robotics infrastructure. Worth noting as a macro trend.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02413

---

**Springer 2026 — "Multi-objective build orientation and topology optimization for cost and time minimization in fiber-reinforced additive manufacturing" (Structural & Multidisciplinary Optimization)**

- **What:** Simultaneous topology + orientation optimization for fiber-reinforced FDM; introduces domain-level orientation variables for full 3D print-plane definition. Differentiable objective functions (build height, surface area, overhang area, support volume).
- **Why it matters:** Academic work, not a product. Uses gradient-based methods similar to orientIQ's approach but couples orientation to topology (a much heavier problem). Confirms the differentiable-objectives direction is live in the literature; orientIQ's lightweight, mesh-only formulation remains faster and library-friendly.
- **Link:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04255-4

---

**Springer 2026 — "Concurrent optimization of print orientation, stacking sequence, and topology for fiber-reinforced additively manufactured components"**

- **What:** Extends orientation + topology co-optimization to fiber stacking sequence for composite AM. Full 3D orientation design variables.
- **Why it matters:** Fiber composites niche; not a direct threat. Confirms academic momentum in simultaneous topology-orientation optimization, which is heavier machinery than orientIQ targets.
- **Link:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-026-04308-8

**Springer 2026 — "A comprehensive review of support structure minimization in additive manufacturing" (Int'l J. Advanced Manufacturing Technology)**

- **Why it matters:** Survey papers signal a maturing field. No new technical threat; useful for citing in future orientIQ documentation.
